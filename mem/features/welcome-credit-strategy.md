Newly approved stylists receive **$25 in welcome store credit** to accelerate their first purchase. This supersedes the earlier no-incentive welcome-offer recommendation; treat the $25 credit as the preferred strategy going forward.

Rationale: $25 is compelling enough to motivate use, especially because stylists do not want to leave available credit unused.

Positioning: frame it as a **benefit of becoming an approved Drop Dead professional**, not a desperate discount to convince someone to buy.

Still to be determined: exact redemption terms and expiration rules.

Priority metrics: time to first purchase, first-order conversion rate, credit redemption rate, subsequent repeat-purchase rate.
